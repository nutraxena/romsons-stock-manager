# Romsons catalogue rules

Catalogue-based product master with explicit list in `product_master.json`.

- Product Wise always shows catalogue products even if quantity is zero.
- Romopad Regular and XL combine in Product Wise; REG/XL remain distinct in Product Size Wise.
- S/M/L/XL/XXL and UNI/SPL merge in Product Wise.
- Krepex 6CM, 8CM, 10CM and 15CM stay separate in Product Wise.
- Unrecognised descriptions are NOT guessed: `Unmatched / Review` row is displayed when present.
- Catalogue names and counts are not proof that all stockist lines have been reconciled. Check the unmatched row before reporting.
- Paramount: Rehab division only.
